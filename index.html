The easy way to book a round of golf.

Fourball is a tee-time booking app being built for Western Cape golf. Golfers find a time and book it in a few taps, the way you would book a padel court, then play and pay at the club as usual. Clubs fill their quiet tee times with no extra admin.

For golf clubs

More rounds on the tee sheet, less work for the pro shop.

  • Fill quiet tee timesGolfers nearby see your open times, and spare spots in a fourball can be taken by other players.
  • Fewer no-showsGolfers get reminders before their round and can cancel in the app, so a freed-up time can be booked again.
  • You stay in controlYou choose which tee times to release and set your own prices, alongside the booking system you use today.
  • Nothing changes at the counterGolfers check in and pay at the pro shop as they do today. Fourball handles the booking, not the money.

How it works

Three steps from open tee time to a round on the tee sheet.

  1. The club releases tee times

    Choose the times you want to open to visitors and show your green fees, including twilight rates and specials.

  2. Golfers book

    They pick a time in the app and add the players in their group. No payment is taken in the app.

  3. They play and pay at the club

    Bookings appear on your start sheet. Golfers check in and pay at the pro shop as usual.

Questions

What clubs ask first.

Is Fourball live?

Not yet. What you can try today is a working prototype with sample courses, golfers and bookings. No real bookings are made.

Does it replace our booking system?

No. It is designed to work alongside what you use today. You decide which tee times go on Fourball and which you keep for members and your own channels.

What does it cost a club?

Fees are agreed with each founding club before the pilot starts. Get in touch and we will talk it through.

What is a founding club?

A small group of Western Cape clubs that will run the first pilot and help shape how Fourball works before it opens more widely.

Where will it be available?

The first clubs will be in the Western Cape. Other regions will follow once the pilot has shown that it works.

Founding clubs

Would this work at your club?

We are looking for a small group of Stellenbosch-area clubs for the first pilot, starting with Stellenbosch University students and local golfers. Have a look at the prototype, then tell us what you think.
